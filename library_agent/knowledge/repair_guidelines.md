# Book repair and replacement guidelines

> Draft for the librarian to edit.

- Loose pages, torn covers and small tears can usually be repaired with book tape.
- Water damage, missing pages or a broken spine usually means the book is replaced.
- A student's past damaged book does **not** mean a newly damaged book cannot be repaired. Each book is assessed on its own.
- Until the librarian decides, a damaged book stays out of circulation.
