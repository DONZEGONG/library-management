# Borrowing limits and loan periods

> Provisional until the committee confirms (PLAN Phase 0).

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Books per student at one time | 2 |
| Loan period | 7 days, due on a Friday |
| Renewals per loan | 1, each adds 7 days |
| Reservation hold | 3 days |
| School is emailed after | 7 days overdue |

Being over the limit, having an unpaid fine, an overdue book, or a past lost or
damaged report does **not** refuse a loan automatically. The agent flags it, and
the committee member who approves decides.
