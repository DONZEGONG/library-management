# Library rules

> Draft for the committee to edit. Numbers marked *provisional* come from the
> Phase 0 defaults and must be confirmed.

- Every book taken out of the library must be registered with the agent or a committee member.
- The loan period is 7 days, Friday to Friday. A book taken on another day is due on the first Friday at least 7 days later.
- A committee member approves every borrowing during the first term.
- A reserved book is held for 3 days (*provisional*). If it is not collected, it goes back on the shelf.
- A book that another student has reserved cannot be renewed.
- Only the librarian may change a due date (for example around festivals), set or waive fines, mark a book lost, correct a record, or delete a record.
- Books must be returned in the condition they were borrowed. Damage is reported at return.
- Students are never blamed in messages. A missing or damaged book is a matter for the librarian to look into.
