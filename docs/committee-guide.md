# Library committee guide

**Sign in** with your school account. You will see Desk, Approvals, Register and Correction log
(and Chat, if the AI chat has been switched on).

## Find a book or student

At the top of **Desk**, type part of a book name, an author, a book ID, a student name or a
student ID, and press **Look up**. Use the IDs it shows in the forms below.

## Register a borrowing

1. On **Desk**, fill in **Borrow**: the book ID (for example `B-101`) and the student ID or name.
2. Press **Send for approval**. The app checks the book and the student's record and creates an
   entry that is **waiting for approval**. Nothing is saved yet.
3. Open **Approvals**. Check the student, book, dates and any red warnings, then press **Approve**.
   The book now shows as Borrowed on the **Register**, due on a Friday.

Warnings like "over the loan limit" or "unpaid fine" don't block the loan. You decide.

## Return a book

Fill in **Return** with the book ID. Pick **Good** or **Damaged**, and describe any damage in
Notes. Approve it on **Approvals**. A damaged book stays out of circulation, and the librarian
decides on any charge.

## Reserve and renew

- **Reserve** saves straight away. The book is held for a few days, or the student joins the
  queue if it is out.
- **Renew** creates an entry to approve. A book someone else has reserved can't be renewed.

If the app can't do something, it says **Not saved** and why, at the top of the page.

## Reject an entry

If anything is wrong, press **Reject** on Approvals and write why. Nothing is saved, and the reason
goes into the correction log for the monthly review.

## Alerts

- Students get a reminder on the due date automatically.
- The day after, the book turns **Overdue** and shows as a **red alert** on the Register.
- After a week overdue, the school office gets one email with the count.

## Ask the librarian for

Fines, changing a due date (for example for a festival), lost books, deleting or correcting records.
Use **Refer to the librarian** on the Desk; it appears on Approvals for the librarian to handle.

## If the AI chat is on

You can also type or press 🎤 on **Chat** and say, for example, *"Asha Rao, grade 7, is borrowing
B-101."* The agent creates the same entries, which still wait on Approvals.
**Never type passwords into the chat.** Use names, grades and student IDs only.
