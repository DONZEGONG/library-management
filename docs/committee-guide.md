# Library committee guide

**Sign in** with your school account. You will see Chat, Approvals, Register and Correction log.

## Register a borrowing

1. On **Chat**, type or press 🎤 and say: *"Asha Rao, grade 7, is borrowing B-101."*
2. Check the text, then press **Send**. The agent checks the student's record and creates an
   action that is **waiting for approval**. Nothing is saved yet.
3. Open **Approvals**. Check the student, book, dates and any red warnings, then press **Approve**.
   The book now shows as Borrowed on the **Register**, due on a Friday.

Warnings like "over the loan limit" or "unpaid fine" don't block the loan. You decide.

## Return a book

Say *"B-101 is back in good condition"* or *"B-101 came back with a torn cover."* Approve it on
**Approvals**. A damaged book stays out of circulation, and the librarian decides on any charge.

## Reserve and renew

- *"Reserve B-201 for Vikram"* saves straight away. The book is held for a few days.
- *"Renew B-101"* creates an action to approve. A book someone else has reserved can't be renewed.

## Reject a suggestion

If anything is wrong, press **Reject** and write why. Nothing is saved, and the reason goes into the
correction log so the agent's mistakes can be reviewed.

## Alerts

- Students get a reminder on the due date automatically.
- The day after, the book turns **Overdue** and shows as a **red alert** on the Register.
- After a week overdue, the school office gets one email with the count.

## Ask the librarian for

Fines, changing a due date (for example for a festival), lost books, deleting or correcting records.
The agent will refuse these and refer them for you.

**Never type passwords into the chat.** Use names, grades and student IDs only.
